# Privacy

Herbal cannot go online: it has no internet permission. Nothing you do in it leaves the phone.

This file describes what is true of the code in this repository *today*. When that changes,
this file changes in the same commit as the code that changed it.

## Today

**The app's manifest declares no permissions.** There is no `INTERNET` permission, so
Android gives the app no way to reach the network. The herb book is inside the APK.

The app keeps one small setting on the phone: whether dark mode is on.

One permission does appear in the built APK, and it is worth naming rather than glossing:
`com.wanderwildwood.yakuso.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION`. It is generated by the
Android build tools, it is defined by this app for this app, and it grants no access to the
device or to you. It exists so that the app's own internal broadcasts cannot be received by
other apps. Nothing in this repository asks for it.

## Links that open in your browser

A source's address on an herb page, and "Feed the llamas" in About (a donation page,
square.link), open in your browser, and only when you press them. The app hands the browser
that one address and nothing else. If you give, the payment is handled on that page by
Square, not by this app.

## Verifying this yourself

- The source is at this repository, and each release is tagged.
- `grep -r uses-permission app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml` shows that nothing is asked for.
- Any APK can be checked with `aapt dump permissions` or by opening it as a zip.
- There is no network code at all. `grep -rnE "URL\(|Socket|HttpURLConnection" app/src`
  finds nothing.

## Who we are

Herbal is made by wander wildwood, an independent developer: one person, not a company. wander wildwood is the data controller for anything the app itself handles, and that is very little. What you do in Herbal stays on your phone. The app sends nothing out: it has no internet permission. The developer receives nothing, so the developer holds no data about you.

## How to contact us

Write to email@wanderthe.dev about your data, this policy or anything else. The same address is in the app, under About.

## Your rights

If you are in the EU, the EEA or the UK, the GDPR gives you the right to:

- see the personal data held about you (access)
- have it corrected (rectification) or deleted (erasure)
- limit how it is used (restriction)
- object to its use (objection)
- get a copy to take elsewhere (portability)
- withdraw your consent at any time, where something rests on it
- complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live

Similar laws elsewhere, such as the CCPA in California, give you the right to know, delete and correct, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of your data. Nothing from Herbal is sold or shared.

Since Herbal keeps what it stores only on your phone, you can exercise most of these rights yourself: clear the app's data in Android's settings, or uninstall the app, which deletes all of it. For anything you cannot do yourself, write to the address above.
